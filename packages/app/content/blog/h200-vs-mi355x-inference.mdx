NVIDIA H200 vs AMD MI355X across 7 shared models on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com). MI355X wins on throughput in 5 of 7, and H200 takes throughput on 2 (Qwen-3.5-397B-A17B and GLM-5). MI355X also wins on cost/Mtok in 5 of 7.

## Key Numbers

- MI355X leads on peak throughput with gpt-oss-120b: **44,999.3 tok/s/GPU** vs 15,202.1 on H200, a 196% gap.
- H200 is cheaper per Mtok only on Qwen-3.5-397B-A17B ($0.22 vs $0.25) and GLM-5 ($0.61 vs $0.98).
- On Kimi-K2.5, MI355X hits 3,510.4 tok/s/GPU vs H200's 1,149.3, a 3.1x difference on the same model.
- H200 wins on GLM-5 throughput (639.5 vs 420.5 tok/s/GPU), one of the few models where it pulls ahead.

MI355X wins on cost in 5 of 7 models. The two exceptions are large, parameter-heavy models (Qwen-3.5-397B-A17B and GLM-5) where H200's higher throughput compensates for its higher hourly rate. On gpt-oss-120b, MI355X is 65% cheaper per Mtok ($0.01 vs $0.03).

## H200 vs MI355X on DeepSeek-R1-0528

MI355X hits 4,222.8 tok/s/GPU vs H200's 2,453.9, a 72% lead. On cost, MI355X is at $0.10/Mtok vs $0.16/Mtok for H200. H200 has slightly better interactivity (9.4 tok/s/user vs 9.2), but the throughput gap is hard to justify.

## Where does H200 still win?

GLM-5 and Qwen-3.5-397B-A17B. On GLM-5, H200 gets 639.5 tok/s/GPU vs MI355X's 420.5, a 52% advantage. Qwen-3.5-397B-A17B is closer: 1,796.7 vs 1,671.5 tok/s/GPU, only 7%. These are both large MoE/dense models where MI355X's framework support is still catching up.
