## Intro

NVIDIA H200 vs AMD MI355X is a cross-vendor, cross-generation matchup. H200 is NVIDIA's last-gen Hopper card; MI355X is AMD's current-gen offering. Both are widely available, so this comparison matters for anyone picking hardware today. We ran both across the full benchmark suite on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com).

## Key Numbers

- MI355X leads on peak throughput in 5 of 7 shared models, often by a wide margin.
- On gpt-oss-120b, MI355X gets **44,999.3 tok/s/GPU** vs H200's 15,202.1 (a 196% gap).
- H200 wins on Qwen-3.5-397B (1,796.7 vs 1,671.5 tok/s/GPU) and GLM-5 (639.5 vs 420.5 tok/s/GPU).
- MI355X is cheaper per Mtok in 5 of 7 models. On MiniMax-M2.5: $0.04 vs $0.12/Mtok.

MI355X wins on cost in 5 of 7 models. The cost advantage ranges from 30% on Llama-3.3-70B to 67% on MiniMax-M2.5. H200 only wins on cost where it also wins on throughput (Qwen-3.5-397B, GLM-5), suggesting those are models where MI355X framework support is still immature.

## Where H200 Still Wins

H200's wins on Qwen-3.5-397B and GLM-5 are interesting. Both are experimental models with limited framework support. On Qwen-3.5-397B, BF16 beats FP8 on AMD hardware, which is unusual and suggests the FP8 kernels aren't optimized yet. On GLM-5, MI355X only manages 420.5 tok/s/GPU with high TPOT (162.5ms). As framework support matures, MI355X will likely close these gaps.
