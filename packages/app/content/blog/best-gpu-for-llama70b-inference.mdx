Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-FP8 is a dense 70B model that fits on a single high-end GPU. At this size, the GPU field is wide and framework choice becomes the differentiator. We benchmarked it across 6 GPUs on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com); for a larger dense model, see our [gpt-oss-120b benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-gptoss120b-inference). Note: this model has been superseded on InferenceX but we continue to benchmark it for reference.

## Key Findings

- NVIDIA B200 on FP4 with TRT hits **12,981.3 tok/s/GPU**, the fastest config for Llama-3.3-70B.
- AMD MI355X on FP4 posts 8,748.9 tok/s/GPU, 67% of the B200 and the top AMD result.
- H200 on FP8 with TRT manages 5,101.6 tok/s/GPU. Half the B200's throughput, but still competitive if you already have the cards.
- H100 on FP8 with vLLM gets 2,568.0 tok/s/GPU. It's the budget pick, running at 20% of the B200.

## Which GPU should you pick for Llama-3.3-70B?

B200 on FP4 with TRT, if throughput is what you care about. It's 48% faster than the MI355X and 2.5x the H200. The catch is TTFT: 18,571.4ms at concurrency 128 is high, likely driven by the high batch size. For AMD, the MI355X on FP4 with vLLM is strong at 8,748.9 tok/s/GPU and much better TTFT (584.6ms). The H100 on FP8 is the budget option at 2,568.0 tok/s/GPU.

## FP4 vs FP8 on Llama-3.3-70B

On the B200, FP4 with TRT reaches 12,981.3 tok/s/GPU while FP8 with TRT gets 9,420.6, a 38% gap. The MI355X tells a similar story: FP4 at 8,748.9 vs FP8 at 7,708.5 tok/s/GPU, a 13% difference. FP4 wins on both NVIDIA and AMD hardware for this model. Hopper cards only have FP8 data so far.

## TRT vs vLLM on Llama-3.3-70B

TRT wins on NVIDIA hardware. On the B200, TRT hits 12,981.3 tok/s/GPU while vLLM gets 10,852.1, a 20% gap. On the H200, TRT leads 5,101.6 vs 3,795.2. vLLM is the only framework tested on AMD and the H100, so there's no direct comparison on those cards.

## NVIDIA B200 vs AMD MI355X on Llama-3.3-70B

B200 wins on throughput at 12,981.3 vs 8,748.9 tok/s/GPU, a 48% gap. But the MI355X has far better TTFT: 584.6ms vs 18,571.4ms. TPOT is nearly identical (63.9ms vs 64.5ms). If your workload needs fast time-to-first-token, the MI355X is the better config. For batch throughput, B200 pulls ahead.

## Fastest time to first token

NVIDIA H100 on FP8 with vLLM at concurrency 64: 466.2ms TTFT. That's the lowest in the dataset, beating the MI355X's 584.6ms by 20%. Throughput is only 2,568.0 tok/s/GPU, so you're trading a lot of throughput for low latency. The MI355X offers a better balance: 584.6ms TTFT with 3.4x the throughput.
