Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-FP8 is a dense 70B parameter model from Meta. At this size it fits on a single GPU in FP8, so the comparison is straightforward: which card pushes the most tokens? We benchmarked it across 6 GPUs on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com); for a larger model, see our [DeepSeek-R1-0528 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-dsr1-inference). Note: this model has been superseded on InferenceX but we continue to benchmark it for reference.

## Key Findings

- NVIDIA B200 on FP4 with TensorRT-LLM hits **12,981.3 tok/s/GPU**, the fastest config for Llama-3.3-70B.
- AMD MI355X on FP4 with vllm reaches 8,748.9 tok/s/GPU, 67% of the B200's throughput at a lower $/Mtok ($0.05 vs $0.04).
- FP4 vs FP8 on B200: FP4 is 38% faster (12,981.3 vs 9,420.6 tok/s/GPU) with TensorRT-LLM.
- NVIDIA H200 on FP8 with TensorRT-LLM still gets 5,101.6 tok/s/GPU, a reasonable last-gen option.

## Which GPU should you pick for Llama-3.3-70B?

NVIDIA B200 on FP4 with TensorRT-LLM. It's 48% faster than the best AMD result. If you need AMD, the MI355X on FP4 with vllm is the best option at 8,748.9 tok/s/GPU, with much better TTFT (584.6ms vs 18,571.4ms on the B200's top config). The H200 with TensorRT-LLM is a decent budget pick at $0.08/Mtok.

## FP4 vs FP8 on Llama-3.3-70B

On the B200, FP4 beats FP8 by 38%: 12,981.3 vs 9,420.6 tok/s/GPU (both with TensorRT-LLM). On AMD MI355X, FP4 is 13% faster: 8,748.9 vs 7,708.5 tok/s/GPU (both with vllm). The FP4 advantage is smaller on AMD than NVIDIA for this model. Cost per token is nearly identical across precisions, so FP4 is the clear pick if your accuracy requirements allow it.

## TensorRT-LLM vs vllm on Llama-3.3-70B

On NVIDIA B200, TensorRT-LLM gets 20% more throughput than vllm (12,981.3 vs 10,852.1 tok/s/GPU). But TensorRT-LLM has very high TTFT at concurrency 128 (18,571.4ms vs 509.2ms for vllm at concurrency 64). On AMD hardware, only vllm is available. Pick TensorRT-LLM for batch throughput, vllm for interactive workloads.

## NVIDIA B200 vs AMD MI355X on Llama-3.3-70B

B200 wins on peak throughput (12,981.3 vs 8,748.9 tok/s/GPU), a 48% edge. MI355X has better TTFT at low concurrency: 584.6ms vs 509.2ms on B200's vllm config (similar), or 584.6ms vs 18,571.4ms on B200's TensorRT-LLM config (large gap). TPOT is similar: 63.9ms B200 vs 64.5ms MI355X. For batch inference the B200 pulls ahead, but at concurrency 64 the gap narrows.

## Fastest time to first token

NVIDIA H100 on FP8 with vllm at concurrency 64: 466.2ms TTFT. That's the lowest across all configs. B200 on FP4 with vllm is close at 509.2ms. The TensorRT-LLM configs on both B200 and H200 have high TTFT (18s+ and 133s respectively) because they're optimized for throughput at higher concurrency.

## Cheapest inference per million tokens

NVIDIA B200 on FP4 at $0.04/Mtok (hyperscaler pricing). AMD MI355X on FP4 and FP8 are both around $0.05/Mtok. The H200 at $0.08/Mtok is 2x more expensive per token. B200 wins on both throughput and cost for this model.
