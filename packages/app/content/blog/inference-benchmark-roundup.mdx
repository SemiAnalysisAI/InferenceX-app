## Overview

This is a cross-model summary of the latest InferenceX benchmark data, covering 7 models across 9 GPUs. Each model has its own detailed article with full tables, FAQ sections, and framework comparisons. The data here uses the best-per-GPU config at the primary 8k/1k sequence length. Explore the full dataset on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com).

## Per-Model Details

### DeepSeek-R1-0528

NVIDIA GB300 on FP4 with dynamo-trt leads at 18,131.6 tok/s/GPU. The disagg + wide expert parallelism setup dominates every top config. AMD MI355X's best result is 4,222.8 tok/s/GPU, a 4.3x gap. See the [full DeepSeek-R1-0528 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-dsr1-inference).

### gpt-oss-120b

B200 on FP4 with TRT reaches 61,612.7 tok/s/GPU on a single GPU, the highest absolute throughput in our suite. MI355X on FP4 with atom gets 73% of that at 44,999.3 tok/s/GPU, the narrowest NVIDIA-AMD gap we've seen. See the [full gpt-oss-120b benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-gptoss120b-inference).

### Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-FP8

A deprecated reference model. B200 on FP4 with TRT tops out at 12,981.3 tok/s/GPU. MI355X on FP8 with vllm actually beats B200 on FP8 with vllm (7,708.5 vs 7,194.4 tok/s/GPU). See the [full Llama-3.3-70B benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-llama70b-inference).

### Qwen-3.5-397B-A17B

B200 on FP8 with sglang leads at 5,709.7 tok/s/GPU. On AMD, BF16 outperforms FP8 (1,671.5 vs 1,288.0 tok/s/GPU on MI355X), which is unusual. See the [full Qwen-3.5-397B benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-qwen3.5-inference).

### Kimi-K2.5

B200 on FP4 with vllm gets 4,020.9 tok/s/GPU. MI355X on FP4 with atom is only 13% behind at 3,510.4 tok/s/GPU. INT4 quantization underperforms FP4 by 2.8x on this model. See the [full Kimi-K2.5 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-kimik2.5-inference).

### MiniMax-M2.5

The one model where AMD wins. MI355X on FP8 with vllm hits 10,391.0 tok/s/GPU, 8% ahead of B200 at 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU. B200 has better latency metrics though. See the [full MiniMax-M2.5 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-minimaxm2.5-inference).

### GLM-5

Limited coverage with only 3 GPUs tested. B200 on FP8 with sglang leads at 1,673.6 tok/s/GPU. H200 and MI355X trail badly. See the [full GLM-5 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-glm5-inference).

## GPU Head-to-Head Comparisons

### AMD MI355X vs NVIDIA B200

The closest matchup in our data. MI355X wins on throughput for 1 out of 7 models (MiniMax-M2.5: 10,391.0 vs 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU). B200 leads on the other 6, by margins ranging from 13% (Kimi-K2.5 with atom vs vllm) to 4.3x (DeepSeek-R1-0528, where MI355X lacks disagg support). On gpt-oss-120b, MI355X gets 73% of B200's throughput (44,999.3 vs 61,612.7 tok/s/GPU), with better TTFT (175.4ms vs 244.5ms). For latency, MI355X often has competitive or lower TTFT at low concurrency. For the full matchup on gpt-oss-120b, see our [gpt-oss-120b benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-gptoss120b-inference).

### NVIDIA B200 vs NVIDIA H200

Generational gap. B200 is 2.1-5.4x faster across all 6 shared models. On gpt-oss-120b, B200 hits 61,612.7 vs H200 at 15,202.1 tok/s/GPU (4.1x). On Qwen-3.5, the gap is smaller: 5,709.7 vs 1,796.7 tok/s/GPU (3.2x). H200 keeps competitive TTFT at low concurrency on some models, but throughput isn't close. If you're choosing between the two, B200 is worth the upgrade unless you're TTFT-bound at low batch sizes. See the [Qwen-3.5 results](/blog/best-gpu-for-qwen3.5-inference) for the tightest matchup.

### AMD MI325X vs AMD MI355X

MI355X leads on every model by 1.1-4.2x. The gap is smallest on Qwen-3.5 (1,671.5 vs 1,406.7 tok/s/GPU at BF16, a 19% difference) and largest on MiniMax-M2.5 (10,391.0 vs 3,950.0 tok/s/GPU, 2.6x). Latency follows the same pattern. MI325X isn't competitive on throughput, but it's available and still runs everything. For the details on MiniMax-M2.5, see our [MiniMax-M2.5 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-minimaxm2.5-inference).

### AMD MI300X vs NVIDIA H200

A cross-vendor older-gen matchup. H200 wins on 4 out of 6 shared models. On DeepSeek-R1-0528, H200 gets 2,325.9 vs MI300X at 790.8 tok/s/GPU (2.9x), thanks to disagg support. On Llama-3.3-70B, MI300X (3,519.8 tok/s/GPU) and H200 with vllm (3,795.2 tok/s/GPU) are close. H200 with TRT (5,101.6 tok/s/GPU) pulls ahead. On MiniMax-M2.5, MI300X (3,503.7 tok/s/GPU) beats H200 (3,370.8 tok/s/GPU) by 4%. For latency, MI300X tends to have lower TTFT at equal concurrency. See our [Llama-3.3-70B benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-llama70b-inference) for the tightest comparison.

### NVIDIA B200 vs NVIDIA H100

H100 is 2-5x slower on throughput across all 4 shared models. On gpt-oss-120b, B200 at 61,612.7 vs H100 at 10,393.9 tok/s/GPU (5.9x). The smallest gap is on MiniMax-M2.5: 9,652.0 vs 2,136.9 tok/s/GPU (4.5x). H100 still has some of the lowest TTFT numbers in our suite at low concurrency (466.2ms on Llama-3.3-70B). If you have existing H100 capacity, it still works for many models. See the [gpt-oss-120b benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-gptoss120b-inference) for the full comparison.

### AMD MI325X vs NVIDIA H200

Close race. H200 wins on 3 of 6 shared models, MI325X on 2, and they're nearly tied on 1. On Qwen-3.5, MI325X on BF16 (1,406.7 tok/s/GPU) beats H200 on FP8 (1,796.7 tok/s/GPU), so H200 still wins there. On MiniMax-M2.5, MI325X (3,950.0 tok/s/GPU) beats H200 (3,370.8 tok/s/GPU) by 17%. On Kimi-K2.5, MI325X on INT4 (298.4 tok/s/GPU) is far behind H200 on INT4 (1,149.3 tok/s/GPU). The right pick depends on your model and framework support. See the [MiniMax-M2.5 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-minimaxm2.5-inference).

## Methodology

Benchmarks are re-run whenever serving frameworks (vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM, etc.) ship updates or when we change benchmark configs, on dedicated hardware with standardized prompts and concurrency sweeps. Results are published on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com). Configs that fail health checks are excluded.
