## Overview

This is a cross-model summary of GPU inference performance from [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com). We benchmark 7 models across up to 9 GPUs, testing multiple frameworks, precisions, and concurrency levels. NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs dominate most models, but AMD MI355X has bright spots. Each model link below goes to a dedicated article with full tables and analysis.

## Per-Model Details

### DeepSeek-R1-0528

NVIDIA GB300 on FP4 with dynamo-trt leads at 18,131.6 tok/s/GPU. Disaggregated prefill is critical on this model, adding 57% throughput on GB300. The NVIDIA-AMD gap is large: best AMD result (MI355X, 4,222.8 tok/s/GPU) is 77% behind. [Full benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-dsr1-inference).

### gpt-oss-120b

NVIDIA B200 on FP4 with TensorRT-LLM hits 61,612.7 tok/s/GPU, the highest throughput of any model we test. AMD MI355X with atom gets 44,999.3 tok/s/GPU (73% of B200) at the same $0.01/Mtok cost. This model runs entirely on FP4. [Full benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-gptoss120b-inference).

### Llama-3.3-70B

A dense 70B model where B200 on FP4 gets 12,981.3 tok/s/GPU. MI355X on FP4 reaches 8,748.9 tok/s/GPU. Simple model, clear NVIDIA lead. [Full benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-llama70b-inference).

### Qwen-3.5-397B

B200 on FP8 dominates at 5,709.7 tok/s/GPU, 3.2x faster than the runner-up. Unusual finding: BF16 beats FP8 on all AMD cards, suggesting immature FP8 support for this model. [Full benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-qwen3.5-inference).

### Kimi-K2.5

B200 on FP4 gets 4,020.9 tok/s/GPU, but MI355X with atom is close at 3,510.4 (87%) and cheaper per token. FP4 is 2.8x faster than INT4 on both vendors. [Full benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-kimik2.5-inference).

### MiniMax-M2.5

The one model where AMD wins. MI355X on FP8 with vllm hits 10,391.0 tok/s/GPU, 8% faster than B200 (9,652.0) and 33% cheaper per token. [Full benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-minimaxm2.5-inference).

### GLM-5

Limited data: only 3 GPUs tested. B200 on FP8 gets 1,673.6 tok/s/GPU. FP8 is faster than FP4 here (immature kernel support). Expensive to serve at $0.32+/Mtok. [Full benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-glm5-inference).

## GPU Head-to-Head Comparisons

### NVIDIA H200 vs AMD MI355X

MI355X wins on throughput in 5 out of 7 shared models, by 116% on average. The exceptions are Qwen-3.5-397B (H200 leads 1,796.7 vs 1,671.5 tok/s/GPU) and GLM-5 (H200 leads 639.5 vs 420.5 tok/s/GPU). On cost, MI355X is cheaper per Mtok in 5 of 7 models. H200 tends to have better TTFT at low concurrencies. For the full MiniMax-M2.5 matchup (MI355X's strongest win), see our [MiniMax-M2.5 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-minimaxm2.5-inference).

### NVIDIA B200 vs AMD MI355X

B200 wins on throughput in 6 out of 7 models, by 124% on average. MiniMax-M2.5 is the exception, where MI355X edges ahead (10,391.0 vs 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU). B200 consistently has lower TPOT. MI355X wins on cost in 2 models (MiniMax-M2.5, Kimi-K2.5). For the closest matchup, see our [Kimi-K2.5 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-kimik2.5-inference).

### NVIDIA B200 vs NVIDIA H200

Generational gap. B200 is 2.5x faster on average across all 7 shared models. H200 doesn't win on any model. H200 still has competitive TTFT at low concurrency on some models (see [Llama-3.3-70B results](/blog/best-gpu-for-llama70b-inference)), but throughput isn't close. B200 also wins on cost/Mtok in every model.

### AMD MI300X vs AMD MI325X

MI325X wins all 6 shared models, but only by 14% on average. Close race: MI325X gets 1,406.7 vs MI300X's 1,314.7 on Qwen-3.5-397B, and 4,673.4 vs 3,519.8 on Llama-3.3-70B. Cost per token is similar. If you have MI300X hardware, upgrading to MI325X gives a modest bump.

### NVIDIA H200 vs AMD MI325X

H200 wins 5 of 6 models by 88% on average throughput. MI325X's only win is on MiniMax-M2.5 (3,950.0 vs 3,370.8 tok/s/GPU). H200 generally has better TTFT at matched concurrency levels. Cost is close on most models.

### NVIDIA H200 vs AMD MI300X

H200 wins 5 of 6 models. MI300X's only win is on MiniMax-M2.5 (3,503.7 vs 3,370.8 tok/s/GPU, a tiny margin). H200 averages 105% more throughput across shared models. MI300X tends to be slightly cheaper per token on the models it competes on. See the [MiniMax-M2.5 results](/blog/best-gpu-for-minimaxm2.5-inference).

### NVIDIA H100 vs AMD MI300X

Surprisingly close. MI300X wins 3 of 4 shared models. On MiniMax-M2.5, MI300X gets 3,503.7 vs H100's 2,136.9 tok/s/GPU. H100 wins on gpt-oss-120b (10,394.0 vs 11,511.9 tok/s/GPU for MI300X, actually MI300X wins there too). On Llama-3.3-70B, MI300X gets 3,519.8 vs H100's 2,568.0. AMD's older card beats NVIDIA's older card on most models.

### NVIDIA H100 vs NVIDIA H200

H200 wins all 4 shared models by 98% on average. On gpt-oss-120b, H200 gets 15,202.1 vs H100's 10,394.0 tok/s/GPU. The gap is largest on DeepSeek-R1-0528: 2,453.9 vs 850.1 tok/s/GPU. H200 is the clear upgrade path. See the [DeepSeek-R1-0528 results](/blog/best-gpu-for-dsr1-inference).

## Methodology

Benchmarks are re-run whenever serving frameworks (vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM, etc.) ship updates or when we change benchmark configs, on dedicated hardware with standardized prompts and concurrency sweeps. Results are published on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com). Configs that fail health checks are excluded.
