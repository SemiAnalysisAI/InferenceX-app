## Overview

This is a cross-model summary of the latest [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com) benchmark data. We test ML inference across 9 GPUs, multiple frameworks, and various precisions. Below you'll find the best GPU for each model, per-model highlights, and GPU head-to-head comparisons drawn from all 7 models in the dataset.

## Per-Model Details

### DeepSeek-R1-0528

NVIDIA GB300 on FP4 with dynamo-trt leads at 18,131.6 tok/s/GPU. The top four GPUs are all NVIDIA Blackwell variants, and they all use FP4 with dynamo-trt. AMD MI355X is the best non-NVIDIA option at 4,222.8 tok/s/GPU, a 4.3x gap. See the [full DeepSeek-R1-0528 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-dsr1-inference).

### gpt-oss-120b

B200 on FP4 with TRT hits 61,612.7 tok/s/GPU, the highest throughput of any model in this roundup. MI355X is competitive at 44,999.3 tok/s/GPU with lower TTFT (175.4ms vs 244.5ms). All GPUs run FP4 on this model. See the [full gpt-oss-120b benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-gptoss120b-inference).

### Llama-3.3-70B (deprecated)

B200 on FP4 with TRT hits 12,981.3 tok/s/GPU. This model has been superseded on the main dashboard, but the data is still useful for hardware comparison. MI355X at 8,748.9 tok/s/GPU has much better TTFT than the B200. See the [full Llama-3.3-70B benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-llama70b-inference).

### Qwen-3.5-397B (experimental)

B200 on FP8 dominates at 5,709.7 tok/s/GPU, 3.2x the next GPU. Only sglang is tested. AMD cards run BF16 and outperform their FP8 configs, which is unusual. See the [full Qwen-3.5-397B benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-qwen3.5-inference).

### Kimi-K2.5 (experimental)

B200 on FP4 with vLLM leads at 4,020.9 tok/s/GPU, but MI355X on FP4 with atom is only 15% behind at 3,510.4. Older AMD cards (MI325X, MI300X) struggle below 300 tok/s/GPU on INT4. See the [full Kimi-K2.5 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-kimik2.5-inference).

### MiniMax-M2.5 (experimental)

The one model where AMD wins. MI355X on FP8 with vLLM hits 10,391.0 tok/s/GPU, edging out the B200 at 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU. B200 has better latency (58.4ms vs 108.3ms TPOT). See the [full MiniMax-M2.5 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-minimaxm2.5-inference).

### GLM-5 (experimental)

Very limited data: 3 GPUs, 1 framework. B200 on FP8 leads at 1,673.6 tok/s/GPU. H200 has unusable TTFT (46,130.9ms) at its best throughput config. See the [full GLM-5 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-glm5-inference).

## GPU Head-to-Head Comparisons

### AMD MI355X vs NVIDIA B200

B200 wins on throughput in 6 out of 7 shared models. The exception is MiniMax-M2.5, where MI355X edges ahead at 10,391.0 vs 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU. The gap varies widely: from 15% on Kimi-K2.5 to 3.2x on DeepSeek-R1-0528. MI355X tends to have lower TTFT at low concurrency. For the closest matchup, see our [Kimi-K2.5 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-kimik2.5-inference).

### AMD MI355X vs NVIDIA H200

MI355X wins throughput in 5 out of 7 shared models, by 1.7x to 3.1x. H200 takes Qwen-3.5-397B (1,796.7 vs 1,671.5 tok/s/GPU, just 7% ahead) and GLM-5 (639.5 vs 420.5 tok/s/GPU). H200 generally has lower TPOT. MI355X is the better throughput play unless you're on one of those two models. For the closest race, see our [Qwen-3.5-397B benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-qwen3.5-inference).

### NVIDIA B200 vs NVIDIA H200

Generational gap. B200 wins all 7 shared models, by 2.5x to 5.5x on throughput. H200 occasionally has competitive TTFT at low concurrency (see [Kimi-K2.5 results](/blog/best-gpu-for-kimik2.5-inference)), but throughput isn't close. If you're choosing between the two, B200 is worth the upgrade unless you're strictly TTFT-bound at low batch sizes.

### AMD MI300X vs AMD MI325X

MI325X wins all 6 shared models, but the margins are small: 4% to 33%. The biggest gap is on Llama-3.3-70B (4,673.4 vs 3,519.8 tok/s/GPU). TPOT tracks similarly across both. MI325X is the better card, but MI300X is close enough that you wouldn't rush to replace it. For the full comparison, see our [Llama-3.3-70B benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-llama70b-inference).

### AMD MI325X vs AMD MI355X

MI355X wins all 6 shared models. Margins range from 19% on Qwen-3.5-397B to 11.8x on Kimi-K2.5. The Kimi-K2.5 gap is extreme because MI355X runs FP4 while MI325X is stuck on INT4. On models with the same precision (MiniMax-M2.5: FP8 on both), the gap is 2.6x. Generational upgrade. See the [MiniMax-M2.5 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-minimaxm2.5-inference).

### AMD MI300X vs NVIDIA H100

Close matchup. MI300X wins 3 out of 4 shared models. On gpt-oss-120b, MI300X leads 11,511.9 vs 10,394.0 tok/s/GPU. H100 wins on DeepSeek-R1-0528 (850.1 vs 790.8 tok/s/GPU), barely. H100 has lower TPOT on most configs. See the [gpt-oss-120b benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-gptoss120b-inference) for the full picture.

### NVIDIA B200 vs NVIDIA H100

B200 wins all 4 shared models by 4.5x to 15.8x. On DeepSeek-R1-0528, the gap is 15.8x (13,429.7 vs 850.1 tok/s/GPU). On gpt-oss-120b, it's 5.9x. This is two generations of improvement. See the [DeepSeek-R1-0528 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-dsr1-inference).

## Methodology

Benchmarks are re-run whenever serving frameworks (vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM, etc.) ship updates or when we change benchmark configs, on dedicated hardware with standardized prompts and concurrency sweeps. Results are published on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com). Configs that fail health checks are excluded.
