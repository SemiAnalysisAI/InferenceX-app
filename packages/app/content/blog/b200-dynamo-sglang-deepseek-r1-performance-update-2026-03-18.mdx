Throughput hovered around 2,230 tok/s/GPU through mid-February, then jumped to 4,242 on March 18. Like the MI355X mori-sglang improvement, this was a step change rather than a gradual ramp.

## What Changed

The specific changes haven't been identified from our benchmarking data alone. The improvement coincides with dynamo-sglang updates for disaggregated prefill on Blackwell hardware.

## Competitive Context

This config (B200 dynamo-sglang FP8 disagg) is now at 4,242 tok/s/GPU, but it's still not the fastest B200 config for DeepSeek-R1-0528. B200 on FP4 with dynamo-trt disagg gets 13,430 tok/s/GPU, over 3x faster. The dynamo-sglang FP8 result is competitive with MI355X's best (4,223 tok/s/GPU on mori-sglang FP4), showing that even B200's secondary configs match AMD's best.
