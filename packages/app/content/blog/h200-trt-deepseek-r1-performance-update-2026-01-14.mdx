Throughput was flat at around 1,320 tok/s/GPU through mid-December, then jumped to 2,454 on January 14. There's a month-long gap between the last December data point and the January result, so the improvement likely came from a TensorRT-LLM update during that period.

## What Changed

The specific changes haven't been identified from our benchmarking data alone. The jump coincides with a TensorRT-LLM release in late December or early January that improved FP8 inference on H200 for large MoE models.

## Competitive Context

Even after this 84% jump, the H200 on TensorRT-LLM FP8 (2,454 tok/s/GPU) is well behind the NVIDIA B200's best config (13,430 tok/s/GPU on FP4 dynamo-trt), and also behind AMD MI355X (4,223 tok/s/GPU on FP4 mori-sglang). At $0.16/Mtok, the H200 is 4x more expensive per token than the B200 ($0.04/Mtok). This improvement keeps H200 relevant for existing deployments but doesn't change the GPU recommendation.
