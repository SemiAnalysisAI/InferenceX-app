GLM-5 is a large model from Zhipu AI with limited GPU coverage on InferenceX so far. Only 3 GPUs and 1 framework (sglang) have been tested, so take these results as early data. We benchmarked it on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com); for broader GPU coverage on a similar-scale model, see our [DeepSeek-R1-0528 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-dsr1-inference). Note: InferenceX support for this model is still early; expect more GPUs and frameworks in future updates.

## Key Findings

- NVIDIA B200 on FP8 hits **1,673.6 tok/s/GPU**, the fastest config for GLM-5.
- NVIDIA H200 on FP8 posts 639.5 tok/s/GPU, 38% of the B200 but with extremely high TTFT (46,130.9ms).
- AMD MI355X on FP8 manages 420.5 tok/s/GPU. TTFT is better at 1,368.1ms, but TPOT is high (162.5ms).

## Which GPU should you pick for GLM-5?

B200 on FP8 with sglang. It's 2.6x faster than the H200 and 4.0x the MI355X. TTFT is also the best at 526.6ms. With only 3 GPUs tested and a single framework, there aren't real alternatives yet. The H200's 46,130.9ms TTFT makes it impractical for interactive use at its best throughput config.

## FP8 vs FP4 on GLM-5

On the B200, FP8 leads at 1,673.6 tok/s/GPU while FP4 gets 1,187.2, a 41% gap favoring FP8. TTFT is similar (526.6ms vs 605.6ms). Only the B200 has both precisions tested, and FP8 wins on both throughput and latency. Other GPUs only have FP8 data.

## NVIDIA B200 vs NVIDIA H200 on GLM-5

B200 wins on throughput and TTFT: 1,673.6 vs 639.5 tok/s/GPU, and 526.6ms vs 46,130.9ms. The H200 has lower TPOT (62.9ms vs 83.7ms), which is the one metric in its favor. That high H200 TTFT is likely driven by the specific config; it may improve with more tuning. For now, B200 is the only reasonable option.

## Fastest time to first token

NVIDIA B200 on FP8 with sglang at concurrency 128: 526.6ms TTFT. That's also the highest-throughput config, so there's no tradeoff on this model. The MI355X comes next at 1,368.1ms, and the H200 is unusable for TTFT at 46,130.9ms.
