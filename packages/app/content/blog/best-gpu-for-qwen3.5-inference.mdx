Qwen-3.5-397B-A17B is a 397B mixture-of-experts model that activates only 17B parameters per token. Despite the low active parameter count, serving it still requires significant GPU memory for the full model weights. We benchmarked it across 5 GPUs on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com); for a denser MoE model, see our [DeepSeek-R1-0528 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-dsr1-inference). Note: InferenceX support for this model is still early; expect more GPUs and frameworks in future updates.

## Key Findings

- NVIDIA B200 on FP8 with sglang hits **5,709.7 tok/s/GPU**, the fastest config for Qwen-3.5-397B.
- NVIDIA H200 on FP8 reaches 1,796.7 tok/s/GPU, just 31% of the B200.
- AMD MI355X on BF16 gets 1,671.5 tok/s/GPU. FP8 on MI355X is actually slower at 1,288.0 tok/s/GPU.
- BF16 beats FP8 on every AMD card tested, which is unusual. MI325X BF16 gets 1,406.7 vs 1,044.9 on FP8.

## Which GPU should you pick for Qwen-3.5-397B?

NVIDIA B200 on FP8 with sglang. It's 3.2x faster than the next best option (B200 on BF16) and 3.4x faster than the best AMD result. If you need AMD, MI355X on BF16 gets 1,671.5 tok/s/GPU at $0.25/Mtok. The B200 at $0.09/Mtok wins on both throughput and cost.

## BF16 vs FP8 on Qwen-3.5-397B

On NVIDIA B200, FP8 is 2.3x faster than BF16 (5,709.7 vs 2,517.2 tok/s/GPU). BF16 has better TPOT though: 27.3ms vs 94.2ms, because the FP8 config runs at concurrency 256 vs 64. On AMD hardware, BF16 is actually faster than FP8 on every card. MI355X BF16 gets 1,671.5 vs 1,288.0 on FP8 (30% gap). This is unusual and likely reflects early FP8 support on AMD for this model.

## NVIDIA B200 vs AMD MI355X on Qwen-3.5-397B

Not close. B200 on FP8 gets 5,709.7 tok/s/GPU vs MI355X's best of 1,671.5 on BF16, a 3.4x gap. TTFT favors MI355X at 299.9ms vs 5,131.8ms on B200 (though B200 runs at much higher concurrency). At concurrency 64, B200 on BF16 gets 2,517.2 tok/s/GPU with 917.1ms TTFT, still faster than MI355X. Cost is $0.09/Mtok vs $0.25/Mtok.

## Fastest time to first token

AMD MI355X on BF16 at concurrency 64: 299.9ms TTFT. MI355X on FP8 is close at 331.3ms. These are well under 1 second, good for interactive chat. NVIDIA B200 on BF16 at concurrency 64 gets 917.1ms TTFT, higher but still reasonable. The B200's FP8 config at concurrency 256 jumps to 5,131.8ms, so pick BF16 at low concurrency if TTFT matters.

## Cheapest inference per million tokens

NVIDIA B200 on FP8 at $0.09/Mtok (hyperscaler pricing). The next cheapest is NVIDIA H200 on FP8 at $0.22/Mtok, which is 2.4x more expensive. AMD MI300X on BF16 at $0.24/Mtok is the cheapest AMD option. B200 wins on cost by a wide margin on this model.
