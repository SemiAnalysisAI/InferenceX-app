Qwen-3.5-397B-A17B is a 397B mixture-of-experts model with only 17B active parameters per token. That low active parameter count makes it fast per-token but still demands a large GPU fleet for the full weights. We benchmarked it across 5 GPUs on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com); for a denser large model, see our [gpt-oss-120b benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-gptoss120b-inference). Note: InferenceX support for this model is still early; expect more GPUs and frameworks in future updates.

## Key Findings

- NVIDIA B200 on FP8 hits **5,709.7 tok/s/GPU**, the fastest config for Qwen-3.5-397B.
- NVIDIA H200 on FP8 comes in at 1,796.7 tok/s/GPU, 31% of the B200. A clear generational gap.
- AMD MI355X on BF16 manages 1,671.5 tok/s/GPU with the lowest TTFT in the dataset (299.9ms).
- Only sglang is tested so far, so framework comparisons aren't possible yet.

## Which GPU should you pick for Qwen-3.5-397B?

B200 on FP8 with sglang is the only config that breaks 5,000 tok/s/GPU. It's 3.2x faster than the H200 and 3.4x the MI355X. TTFT is high (5,131.8ms) because the best throughput comes at concurrency 256. For lower latency on AMD, the MI355X on BF16 gets 1,671.5 tok/s/GPU with just 299.9ms TTFT. Coverage is thin right now, so these rankings may shift as more frameworks and GPUs are tested.

## FP8 vs BF16 on Qwen-3.5-397B

On AMD, BF16 beats FP8. The MI355X on BF16 hits 1,671.5 tok/s/GPU vs 1,288.0 on FP8, a 30% improvement. Same pattern on the MI325X: 1,406.7 (BF16) vs 1,044.9 (FP8). NVIDIA cards only have FP8 data. The BF16 advantage on AMD is unusual and worth watching as more configs are added.

## NVIDIA B200 vs NVIDIA H200 on Qwen-3.5-397B

B200 is 3.2x faster: 5,709.7 vs 1,796.7 tok/s/GPU. The H200 has much better TTFT though, at 626.8ms vs 5,131.8ms. TPOT also favors the H200: 39.6ms vs 94.2ms. If you're running interactive workloads at low concurrency, the H200 offers better per-request latency. For throughput, B200 is the clear winner.

## Fastest time to first token

AMD MI355X on BF16 with sglang at concurrency 64: 299.9ms TTFT. That's less than half the H200's 626.8ms and 17x faster than the B200's 5,131.8ms. Throughput at this config is 1,671.5 tok/s/GPU. For interactive chat where first-token latency matters more than batch throughput, the MI355X is the right pick.
