gpt-oss-120b is a dense 120B parameter model. Because it's smaller than the massive MoE models, more GPUs can serve it without aggressive quantization, and framework choice becomes the differentiator. We benchmarked it across 7 GPUs on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com); for a much larger MoE model, see our [DeepSeek-R1-0528 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-dsr1-inference).

## Key Findings

- NVIDIA B200 on FP4 with TensorRT-LLM hits **61,612.7 tok/s/GPU**, the fastest config we've tested for gpt-oss-120b.
- NVIDIA GB200 on FP4 with dynamo-trt reaches 53,952.5 tok/s/GPU, 88% of the B200.
- AMD MI355X on FP4 with atom gets 44,999.3 tok/s/GPU. That's 73% of the B200 at a lower cost per token ($0.01 vs $0.01/Mtok).
- All top GPUs run FP4 exclusively. No FP8 configs appear in the data for this model.
- NVIDIA H100 still manages 10,394.0 tok/s/GPU on FP4, making it viable for cost-conscious deployments.

## Which GPU should you pick for gpt-oss-120b?

NVIDIA B200 on FP4 with TensorRT-LLM. It's 14% faster than the GB200 and 37% faster than the MI355X, with the lowest TTFT of any high-throughput config (244.5ms). If you need an AMD option, the MI355X on FP4 with atom delivers 44,999.3 tok/s/GPU at the same $0.01/Mtok price point. The H200 works but you're leaving 4x throughput on the table compared to the B200.

## TensorRT-LLM vs vllm vs atom

On NVIDIA B200, TensorRT-LLM beats vllm by 20% (61,612.7 vs 51,523.0 tok/s/GPU). vllm has better TTFT though: 172.6ms vs 244.5ms. On AMD MI355X, atom beats vllm by 17% (44,999.3 vs 38,532.2 tok/s/GPU). atom also has a TTFT edge on MI355X: 175.4ms vs 268.7ms. Pick TensorRT-LLM/atom for throughput, vllm if you need lower latency at lower concurrency.

## NVIDIA B200 vs AMD MI355X on gpt-oss-120b

The B200 wins on peak throughput (61,612.7 vs 44,999.3 tok/s/GPU), a 37% edge. TTFT is close: 244.5ms on B200 vs 175.4ms on MI355X (MI355X actually wins there). TPOT favors B200: 36.7ms vs 25.1ms at their respective best configs. Cost per million tokens is nearly identical at $0.01/Mtok for both. If you're optimizing for throughput, B200 is the pick. If TTFT matters, MI355X is competitive.

## Fastest time to first token

NVIDIA B200 on FP4 with vllm at concurrency 128: 172.6ms TTFT. AMD MI355X on FP4 with atom is close at 175.4ms. Both are well under 300ms, which is good enough for interactive chat. The GB200 has much higher TTFT (5,027.7ms) because it runs at concurrency 512. If TTFT matters more than throughput, B200 with vllm or MI355X with atom at concurrency 128 are the configs to run.

## Cheapest inference per million tokens

NVIDIA B200 on FP4 at $0.01/Mtok (hyperscaler pricing). AMD MI355X on FP4 is also $0.01/Mtok, making it the cheapest AMD option. The MI325X, MI300X, H200, and H100 are all around $0.03/Mtok, roughly 3x more expensive per token despite much lower throughput. On this model, the newer GPUs win on both throughput and cost.
