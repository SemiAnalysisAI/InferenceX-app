gpt-oss-120b is a dense 120B parameter model. At this size, most current-gen GPUs can load it in FP4, so the field is wide open. We benchmarked it across 7 GPUs on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com); for a much larger MoE model, see our [DeepSeek-R1-0528 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-dsr1-inference).

## Key Findings

- NVIDIA B200 on FP4 hits **61,612.7 tok/s/GPU** with TensorRT-LLM, the fastest config for this model.
- NVIDIA GB200 on FP4 with disagg reaches 53,952.5 tok/s/GPU, 12.4% behind the B200.
- AMD MI355X on FP4 with atom posts 44,999.3 tok/s/GPU, 73% of the B200 and the best AMD result.
- Even the H100 manages 10,394.0 tok/s/GPU on FP4 with vLLM. Three generations back and still in five figures.

## Which GPU should you pick for gpt-oss-120b?

B200 on FP4 with TRT is the clear winner at 61,612.7 tok/s/GPU, and TTFT is solid at 244.5ms. If you need an AMD option, the MI355X on FP4 with atom gets 73% of that throughput at 44,999.3 tok/s/GPU, with lower TTFT (175.4ms). The GB200 trails the B200 on throughput but has the lowest TPOT in the dataset at 14.7ms, which matters for streaming use cases.

## TRT vs vLLM vs atom vs dynamo-trt

Split results. TRT wins on the B200 (61,612.7 tok/s/GPU) while atom wins on the MI355X (44,999.3 vs 38,532.2 for vLLM, a 17% edge). vLLM is the only framework tested on the H100, MI325X, and MI300X, so there's no direct comparison on those cards. On the GB200, dynamo-trt with disagg hits 53,952.5 tok/s/GPU. Framework matters here, especially on AMD.

## Does disaggregated prefill help?

Not clearly on this model. The best non-disagg config (B200 at 61,612.7 tok/s/GPU) actually beats the best disagg config (GB200 at 53,952.5 tok/s/GPU). That said, these are different GPUs, so it's not an apples-to-apples test. The GB200 disagg result has great TPOT (14.7ms) but high TTFT (5,027.7ms). Without a same-GPU disagg vs. non-disagg comparison, the verdict is inconclusive.

## NVIDIA B200 vs AMD MI355X on gpt-oss-120b

B200 wins on throughput (61,612.7 vs 44,999.3 tok/s/GPU), a 37% gap. TTFT is close: 244.5ms for the B200, 175.4ms for the MI355X. TPOT favors the MI355X too (25.1ms vs 36.7ms). If your workload is latency-sensitive with short outputs, the MI355X is competitive. For batch throughput, B200 is still the pick.

## Fastest time to first token

NVIDIA B200 on FP4 with vLLM at concurrency 128: 172.6ms TTFT. That's 29% faster than the MI355X's 175.4ms on atom, and far below the B200's own TRT result (244.5ms). Throughput at this config is 51,523.0 tok/s/GPU, so you're not giving up much. If TTFT matters, vLLM on the B200 is the config to run.
