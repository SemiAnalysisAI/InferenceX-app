Kimi-K2.5 is a large MoE model from Moonshot AI. GPU coverage is still limited, with 5 GPUs tested so far. We benchmarked it on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com); for another large MoE, see our [DeepSeek-R1-0528 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-dsr1-inference). Note: InferenceX support for this model is still early; expect more GPUs and frameworks in future updates.

## Key Findings

- NVIDIA B200 on FP4 with vLLM hits **4,020.9 tok/s/GPU**, the fastest config for Kimi-K2.5.
- AMD MI355X on FP4 with atom reaches 3,510.4 tok/s/GPU, 87% of the B200. Very competitive.
- NVIDIA H200 on INT4 posts 1,149.3 tok/s/GPU. A steep drop from the B200 but the best Hopper result.
- AMD MI325X and MI300X both sit below 300 tok/s/GPU on INT4. These older AMD cards struggle with this model.

## Which GPU should you pick for Kimi-K2.5?

B200 on FP4 with vLLM is the top pick at 4,020.9 tok/s/GPU, with good TTFT (645.9ms) and the lowest TPOT in the dataset (34.1ms). On AMD, the MI355X on FP4 with atom is surprisingly close at 3,510.4 tok/s/GPU. TPOT is higher (80.4ms) but TTFT is nearly identical. The H200 on INT4 is a distant third at 1,149.3 tok/s/GPU. Older AMD cards aren't viable for this model.

## FP4 vs INT4 on Kimi-K2.5

Big gap. On the B200, FP4 gets 4,020.9 tok/s/GPU while INT4 only manages 1,419.3, a 2.8x difference. On the MI355X, FP4 at 3,510.4 vs INT4 at 1,223.0 tells the same story, a 2.9x gap. FP4 is the clear winner on every GPU that supports it.

## vLLM vs atom on Kimi-K2.5

atom wins on the MI355X at 3,510.4 vs 2,697.4 tok/s/GPU for vLLM (FP4), a 30% edge. vLLM wins on the B200 and is the only option on H200, MI325X, and MI300X. If you're on AMD MI355X, atom is worth testing.

## NVIDIA B200 vs AMD MI355X on Kimi-K2.5

B200 edges ahead on throughput (4,020.9 vs 3,510.4 tok/s/GPU), a 15% gap. TTFT is nearly identical: 645.9ms vs 649.6ms. The B200 has a big TPOT advantage: 34.1ms vs 80.4ms. For streaming workloads where per-token latency matters, B200 is the stronger choice. For raw throughput, the gap is small enough that price could decide.

## Fastest time to first token

NVIDIA B200 on INT4 with vLLM at concurrency 64: 457.5ms TTFT. That's 29% faster than the B200's FP4 result (645.9ms), but throughput drops to 1,419.3 tok/s/GPU, about one-third the FP4 config. On the MI355X, the best TTFT is 485.8ms (FP4, vLLM), close to the B200 and with better throughput (2,697.4 tok/s/GPU).
