Kimi-K2.5 is a large model from Moonshot AI. GPU selection here is interesting because AMD's MI355X with atom gets close to the B200 on throughput, and actually wins on cost per token. We benchmarked it across 5 GPUs on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com); for another experimental model, see our [Qwen-3.5-397B benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-qwen3.5-inference). Note: InferenceX support for this model is still early; expect more GPUs and frameworks in future updates.

## Key Findings

- NVIDIA B200 on FP4 with vllm hits **4,020.9 tok/s/GPU**, the fastest config for Kimi-K2.5.
- AMD MI355X on FP4 with atom reaches 3,510.4 tok/s/GPU, 87% of the B200, at a lower $/Mtok ($0.12 vs $0.13).
- FP4 vs INT4: FP4 is 2.8x faster on B200 (4,020.9 vs 1,419.3 tok/s/GPU) and 2.9x faster on MI355X.
- Older AMD cards (MI325X, MI300X) on INT4 drop below 300 tok/s/GPU. Not viable for production.

## Which GPU should you pick for Kimi-K2.5?

NVIDIA B200 on FP4 with vllm for peak throughput. AMD MI355X on FP4 with atom is the better value play at $0.12/Mtok vs $0.13/Mtok, getting 87% of the B200's throughput. TTFT is nearly identical between them (645.9ms vs 649.6ms). If you can only run INT4, the H200 at 1,149.3 tok/s/GPU works but costs 2.6x more per token than the B200 on FP4.

## FP4 vs INT4 on Kimi-K2.5

On B200, FP4 is 2.8x faster than INT4 (4,020.9 vs 1,419.3 tok/s/GPU). On MI355X, FP4 with atom is 2.9x faster than INT4 with vllm (3,510.4 vs 1,223.0 tok/s/GPU). Cost per token is also far better with FP4: $0.13 vs $0.38 on B200. FP4 wins on every metric for this model.

## atom vs vllm on Kimi-K2.5

On AMD MI355X with FP4, atom beats vllm by 30% on throughput: 3,510.4 vs 2,697.4 tok/s/GPU. atom runs at concurrency 128 vs 64 for vllm, which partly explains the gap. TPOT is higher on atom (80.4ms vs 52.3ms), so vllm is better for latency-sensitive single-request workloads. For batch throughput, atom is the pick.

## NVIDIA B200 vs AMD MI355X on Kimi-K2.5

Closer than most models. B200 gets 4,020.9 vs MI355X's 3,510.4 tok/s/GPU (15% edge). TTFT is a tie: 645.9ms vs 649.6ms. TPOT favors B200: 34.1ms vs 80.4ms. But MI355X is cheaper per token: $0.12 vs $0.13/Mtok. If you're cost-sensitive and can tolerate higher per-request latency, MI355X is competitive.

## Fastest time to first token

NVIDIA B200 on INT4 with vllm at concurrency 64: 457.5ms TTFT. AMD MI355X on FP4 with vllm is close at 485.8ms. All the FP4 and INT4 configs at concurrency 64 cluster between 457ms and 520ms. The only high-TTFT configs are MI325X and MI300X at 1,649ms and 1,739ms respectively.

## Cheapest inference per million tokens

AMD MI355X on FP4 with atom at $0.12/Mtok (hyperscaler pricing). NVIDIA B200 on FP4 is slightly more at $0.13/Mtok. This is one of the few models where AMD beats NVIDIA on cost. INT4 configs are 2-3x more expensive per token despite lower throughput.
