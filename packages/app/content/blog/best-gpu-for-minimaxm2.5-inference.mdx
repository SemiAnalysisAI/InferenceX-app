MiniMax-M2.5 is a MoE model where AMD MI355X pulls ahead of NVIDIA B200 on throughput, one of the few models in our suite where that happens. Six GPUs are tested, all on FP8, with vllm and atom as the available frameworks. We benchmarked it on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com); for a model where NVIDIA dominates instead, see our [DeepSeek-R1-0528 benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-dsr1-inference). Note: InferenceX support for this model is still early; expect more GPUs and frameworks in future updates.

## Key Findings

- AMD MI355X on FP8 with vllm hits **10,391.0 tok/s/GPU**, the fastest config for MiniMax-M2.5.
- NVIDIA B200 on FP8 with vllm comes in at 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU, just 7% behind MI355X.
- MI355X on atom also performs well at 8,395.9 tok/s/GPU, giving AMD two of the top three configs.
- H100 on FP8 with vllm still delivers 2,136.9 tok/s/GPU, though that's 79% slower than MI355X.

## Which GPU should you pick for MiniMax-M2.5?

MI355X on FP8 with vllm at 10,391.0 tok/s/GPU. This is one of the few models where AMD leads NVIDIA on throughput. B200 on FP8 is close at 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU with better TPOT (58.4ms vs 108.3ms), so if per-token latency matters more than aggregate throughput, B200 is the pick. On older AMD hardware, MI325X on FP8 still gets 3,950.0 tok/s/GPU.

## vllm vs atom on AMD MI355X

vllm (10,391.0 tok/s/GPU) beats atom (8,395.9 tok/s/GPU) by 24% on throughput. TPOT is worse on vllm though: 108.3ms vs 67.4ms. TTFT is similar (747.7ms vs 512.2ms). vllm runs at concurrency 256, atom at 128. If you're optimizing for throughput at scale, vllm is the framework to run. For lower-latency serving, atom's TPOT is 38% better.

## AMD MI355X vs NVIDIA B200 on MiniMax-M2.5

MI355X leads on throughput: 10,391.0 vs 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU. B200 has the better latency profile, with TTFT at 436.4ms (vs 747.7ms) and TPOT at 58.4ms (vs 108.3ms). If your workload is batch inference or high-concurrency API serving, MI355X wins. For interactive chat with strict latency SLAs, B200 is the safer choice despite lower throughput.

## Fastest time to first token

NVIDIA H200 on FP8 with vllm at concurrency 128: 340.4ms TTFT with 3,370.8 tok/s/GPU. B200 is close at 436.4ms. H100 has 31,646.2ms TTFT at its top-throughput config, so avoid that one for latency-sensitive use. At concurrency 64, MI325X gives 587.1ms TTFT with 3,950.0 tok/s/GPU.
