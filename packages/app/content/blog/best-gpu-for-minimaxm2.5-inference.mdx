MiniMax-M2.5 is a mixture-of-experts model where AMD hardware actually leads on throughput. That's unusual in our benchmarks and makes this model worth watching. We tested it across 6 GPUs on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com); for comparison with another MoE, see our [Qwen-3.5-397B benchmarks](/blog/best-gpu-for-qwen3.5-inference). Note: InferenceX support for this model is still early; expect more GPUs and frameworks in future updates.

## Key Findings

- AMD MI355X on FP8 with vLLM hits **10,391.0 tok/s/GPU**, the fastest config for MiniMax-M2.5.
- NVIDIA B200 on FP8 is close behind at 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU, 93% of the MI355X.
- AMD MI325X posts 3,950.0 tok/s/GPU on FP8, which is 38% of the MI355X. Last-gen AMD still holds up.
- H100 on FP8 manages 2,136.9 tok/s/GPU but with extremely high TTFT (31,646.2ms).

## Which GPU should you pick for MiniMax-M2.5?

MI355X on FP8 with vLLM takes the lead at 10,391.0 tok/s/GPU. One of the few models where AMD beats NVIDIA on throughput. The B200 is only 7% behind at 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU and has better TPOT (58.4ms vs 108.3ms), so it's the stronger choice for streaming. If you need a budget option, the H200 at 3,370.8 tok/s/GPU has the lowest TTFT in the set (340.4ms).

## vLLM vs atom on MiniMax-M2.5

vLLM wins on the MI355X: 10,391.0 vs 8,395.9 tok/s/GPU for atom, a 24% gap. Both use FP8. vLLM is the only framework tested on all other GPUs. On this model, vLLM is the clear pick.

## AMD MI355X vs NVIDIA B200 on MiniMax-M2.5

MI355X wins on throughput (10,391.0 vs 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU), one of the rare cases where AMD leads. B200 wins on latency: 436.4ms TTFT vs 747.7ms, and 58.4ms TPOT vs 108.3ms. For latency-sensitive workloads, the B200 is better despite slightly lower throughput. For batch processing, MI355X has the edge.

## Fastest time to first token

NVIDIA H200 on FP8 with vLLM at concurrency 128: 340.4ms TTFT. The B200 is close at 436.4ms. The MI355X, despite leading on throughput, has higher TTFT at 747.7ms. If your workload prioritizes fast first responses, the H200 is the config to pick. Throughput is 3,370.8 tok/s/GPU, about a third of the MI355X.
