B200 vs MI355X across 7 shared models on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com). B200 wins on throughput in 6 of 7. MI355X wins on cost/Mtok in 3 of 7, and takes the throughput crown on MiniMax-M2.5.

## Key Numbers

- B200 (TRT) FP4 leads peak throughput on gpt-oss-120b: **61,612.7 tok/s/GPU** vs 44,999.3 on MI355X (Atom) FP4.
- MI355X (vLLM) FP8 beats B200 on MiniMax-M2.5 at 8k/1k: 10,391.0 vs 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU, and it's 29% cheaper per Mtok ($0.04 vs $0.06).
- B200 (Dynamo TRT) FP4 hits 13,429.7 tok/s/GPU on DeepSeek-R1, 3.2x what MI355X (MoRI SGLang) FP4 manages (4,222.8).
- MI355X wins on cost/Mtok for Kimi-K2.5 ($0.12 vs $0.13) and gpt-oss-120b ($0.01 vs $0.01, marginal), even losing on raw throughput.

## DeepSeek-R1-0528

The largest gap in the dataset. B200 (Dynamo TRT) FP4 with disaggregated prefill pulls 13,429.7 tok/s/GPU. MI355X's best config, MoRI SGLang FP4 with disagg, reaches 4,222.8. That's a 3.2x throughput gap, which maps to a 2.4x cost gap ($0.04 vs $0.10/Mtok).

MI355X's composability problem shows here. Its FP4 disagg results (MoRI SGLang) nearly doubled since February 2026, jumping from 1,733.5 to 4,222.8 tok/s/GPU. But B200 started from a higher baseline and the gap remains wide.

## gpt-oss-120b

B200 (TRT) FP4 hits 61,612.7 tok/s/GPU. MI355X (Atom) FP4 reaches 44,999.3. That's a 37% gap, which is the smallest throughput margin in this dataset by percentage after MiniMax-M2.5.

Cost tells a closer story: B200 at $0.01/Mtok, MI355X at $0.01/Mtok. The difference is fractions of a penny ($0.0088 vs $0.0091). If you're running gpt-oss-120b and MI355X cards are easier to get, the cost math works.

## Which GPU should you pick?

B200 if you need throughput. It wins 6 of 7 models, and the gaps are large on MoE models like DeepSeek-R1 (3.2x) and GLM-5 (4x). TRT and Dynamo TRT are the best frameworks on B200 for most models.

MI355X if you're cost-sensitive on specific models. It's cheaper per Mtok on MiniMax-M2.5, Kimi-K2.5, and gpt-oss-120b (barely). The Atom framework has been a big addition for MI355X, pushing it closer to B200 on several models. If your workload is MiniMax-M2.5, MI355X is the pick.

## FP4 vs FP8

On B200, FP4 consistently beats FP8 by large margins. DeepSeek-R1: 13,429.7 vs 5,856.9 tok/s/GPU (2.3x). gpt-oss-120b: 61,612.7 vs not available at 8k/1k (only FP4 tested). Llama-3.3-70B: 12,981.3 vs 9,420.6 (1.4x).

On MI355X, FP4 also leads but the gap is smaller. DeepSeek-R1: 4,222.8 vs 3,815.3 (1.1x). Kimi-K2.5: 3,510.4 vs not tested. The composability issue on MI355X means FP4 gains aren't as large with disagg enabled.

## Frameworks: TRT vs SGLang vs vLLM vs Atom

B200's best results come from TRT (gpt-oss-120b, Llama-3.3-70B) or Dynamo TRT (DeepSeek-R1). SGLang is competitive on B200 for Qwen-3.5 and GLM-5 where TRT configs aren't tested yet.

MI355X's best results come from Atom (gpt-oss-120b, Kimi-K2.5, DeepSeek-R1 non-disagg) and MoRI SGLang (DeepSeek-R1 disagg). vLLM wins on MiniMax-M2.5 and Llama-3.3-70B. Framework diversity on MI355X is improving.
