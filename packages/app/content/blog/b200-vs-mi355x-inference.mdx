NVIDIA B200 vs AMD MI355X across 7 shared models on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com). B200 wins on throughput in 6 of 7. MI355X takes MiniMax-M2.5 by 8%. On cost/Mtok, B200 is cheaper in 5 of 7, while MI355X undercuts on Kimi-K2.5 and MiniMax-M2.5.

## Key Numbers

- B200 leads on peak throughput with gpt-oss-120b: **61,612.7 tok/s/GPU** vs 44,999.3 on MI355X, a 37% gap.
- MI355X wins MiniMax-M2.5 at 10,391.0 vs 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU, the only model where it beats B200 on throughput.
- At $0.12/Mtok on Kimi-K2.5, MI355X is 13% cheaper than B200's $0.13/Mtok (hyperscaler pricing).
- On DeepSeek-R1-0528, B200 hits 14,952.0 tok/s/GPU vs MI355X's 4,222.8, a 3.5x difference.

## Performance Over Time (gpt-oss-120b)

Both GPUs have improved on gpt-oss-120b since September 2025. B200 went from 10,914 tok/s/GPU to 61,613 tok/s/GPU, a 5.6x gain over five months driven by TRT upgrades and concurrency scaling. MI355X climbed from 21,244 to 38,532 tok/s/GPU on vLLM FP4. The Atom framework pushed MI355X to 44,999 in a single-date run (January 2026) but hasn't been re-benchmarked since.

## Where MI355X wins

MiniMax-M2.5 is the only throughput win for MI355X: 10,391.0 vs 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU, both on FP8 vLLM at concurrency 256. MI355X also has lower TPOT on gpt-oss-120b (25.1ms vs 36.7ms) and lower TTFT on Qwen-3.5 (299.9ms vs 5,131.8ms). On cost, MI355X undercuts B200 on Kimi-K2.5 ($0.12 vs $0.13/Mtok) and MiniMax-M2.5 ($0.04 vs $0.06/Mtok).

## B200 vs MI355X on DeepSeek-R1-0528

B200 hits 14,952.0 tok/s/GPU on dynamo-trt FP4 with disaggregated prefill at concurrency 4,301. MI355X reaches 4,222.8 tok/s/GPU on mori-sglang FP4 disagg at concurrency 2,048. That's a 3.5x gap, the largest among shared models. B200's TPOT is 36.9ms vs MI355X's 108.1ms. MI355X's TTFT is very high at 71,289ms because it's running at max concurrency; at lower concurrency (sglang FP4, conc 64), MI355X gets 656ms TTFT but only 1,952 tok/s/GPU throughput.

## Fastest time to first token

MI355X on Qwen-3.5-397B-A17B BF16 with SGLang at concurrency 64: 299.9ms TTFT. B200's best TTFT on the same model is 917.1ms (also BF16 SGLang, conc 64). On gpt-oss-120b the gap narrows: B200 at 172.6ms vs MI355X at 175.4ms. B200 tends to run at higher concurrency to maximize throughput, which inflates TTFT. If you need low TTFT, lower-concurrency MI355X configs are competitive on several models.
