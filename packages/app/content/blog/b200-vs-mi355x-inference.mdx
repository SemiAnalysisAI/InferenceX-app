NVIDIA B200 vs AMD MI355X across 7 shared models on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com). B200 wins on throughput in 6 of 7. MI355X takes MiniMax-M2.5 (10,391.0 vs 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU). On cost per Mtok, B200 wins 4 and MI355X wins 3.

## Key Numbers

- B200 leads on gpt-oss-120b: **61,612.7 tok/s/GPU** vs MI355X's 44,999.3, a 37% gap.
- MI355X beats B200 on MiniMax-M2.5: 10,391.0 vs 9,652.0 tok/s/GPU, an 8% edge.
- On DeepSeek-R1-0528, B200 is 3.2x faster (13,429.7 vs 4,222.8 tok/s/GPU) and 59% cheaper per Mtok ($0.04 vs $0.10).
- MI355X wins on cost for Kimi-K2.5 ($0.12 vs $0.13/Mtok), MiniMax-M2.5 ($0.04 vs $0.06), and Llama-3.3-70B ($0.05 vs $0.04).

## B200 vs MI355X on DeepSeek-R1-0528

B200 hits 13,429.7 tok/s/GPU, 3.2x MI355X's 4,222.8. Cost is $0.04/Mtok vs $0.10/Mtok. This is one of the biggest gaps in the dataset. DeepSeek-R1-0528 is a 671B MoE model where B200's memory bandwidth and FP4 support with TensorRT-LLM give it a massive advantage.

## Where MI355X wins

MiniMax-M2.5. MI355X gets 10,391.0 tok/s/GPU vs B200's 9,652.0, an 8% lead. Cost is $0.04/Mtok vs $0.06/Mtok. On Kimi-K2.5, B200 wins throughput (4,020.9 vs 3,510.4) but MI355X is slightly cheaper per Mtok ($0.12 vs $0.13). If you're running MiniMax-M2.5 at scale, MI355X is the better deal.
