## Introduction

NVIDIA B200 vs AMD MI355X across 7 shared models on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com). B200 wins on peak throughput in 6 of 7 models. MI355X wins on cost/Mtok in 3 of 7 (gpt-oss-120b, MiniMax-M2.5, and Kimi-K2.5), and it actually beats the B200 on raw throughput for MiniMax-M2.5. The average throughput gap across all models is 124%, but that number is skewed by DeepSeek R1 and Qwen 3.5 where B200's disagg and FP4 advantages are massive.

The picture changes when you account for pricing. MI355X's lower hyperscaler $/hr rate means it can compete on $/Mtok even when throughput is 25-30% behind. On dense models (gpt-oss-120b, Llama 70B), MI355X is surprisingly close on both throughput and cost. On large MoE models (DeepSeek R1, Qwen 3.5, GLM-5), the gap blows open because AMD's FP4 disagg+wideEP composability is still immature.

## Key Numbers

- B200 leads on peak throughput: **61,612.7 tok/s/GPU** on gpt-oss-120b FP4 TRT vs MI355X's 44,999.3 on Atom.
- MI355X is cheaper on MiniMax-M2.5: $0.04/Mtok vs $0.06/Mtok, while also delivering 8% more throughput.
- On DeepSeek R1, B200 with Dynamo TRT disagg hits 13,429.7 tok/s/GPU. MI355X's best (MoRI SGLang disagg) manages 4,222.8, a 3.2x gap.
- Llama 70B is the closest race: B200 at 12,981.3 tok/s/GPU vs MI355X at 8,748.9, only 48% apart, and MI355X wins on TTFT (584ms vs 18,571ms).

B200 is 3.2x faster on DeepSeek R1. The story here is entirely about disaggregated serving maturity. B200 runs Dynamo TRT-LLM with FP4 disagg, a battle-tested stack that NVIDIA has been optimizing for months. MI355X runs MoRI SGLang disagg, which has improved rapidly (throughput went from 1,734 to 4,223 tok/s/GPU between February and March 2026) but still has composability issues with FP4+wideEP.

The TTFT gap is brutal: 4.1 seconds for B200 vs 71.3 seconds for MI355X. At $0.04/Mtok vs $0.10/Mtok, B200 is 2.4x cheaper per token too. If you're serving DeepSeek R1 and can get B200s, there's no reason to pick MI355X today.

MI355X does have an interesting non-disagg option: Atom at 3,652.0 tok/s/GPU with a 1,021ms TTFT. That's a 70x improvement in TTFT over disagg, though throughput drops 14%. Atom is worth watching as AMD's alternative to disagg on models where their distributed stack isn't yet competitive.

MI355X wins on cost for 3 of 7 models (gpt-oss-120b, MiniMax-M2.5, Kimi-K2.5). The pattern is clear: on dense models and MoE models where both GPUs run the same precision, MI355X's lower $/hr compensates for its throughput deficit. On models where B200 can run FP4 disagg and MI355X can't match it, B200 wins on cost too because throughput scales faster than the price difference.

For batch inference on gpt-oss-120b or MiniMax-M2.5, MI355X is the better economics. For anything MoE at scale (DeepSeek R1, Qwen 3.5), B200's software stack advantage translates directly into lower cost per token.

## Methodology

Benchmarks are re-run whenever serving frameworks (vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM, Atom, etc.) ship updates or when we change benchmark configs, on dedicated hardware with standardized prompts and concurrency sweeps. Results are published on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com). Configs that fail health checks are excluded.
