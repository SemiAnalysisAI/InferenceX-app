AMD MI355X on mori-sglang just went from 1,893.2 to 4,222.8 tok/s/GPU on DeepSeek-R1-0528 inference, a 2.2x improvement in 10 days. The gains come from mori-sglang's FP4 disaggregated prefill pipeline on AMD hardware.

Performance was flat at around 1,750 tok/s/GPU from early February through early March, then jumped to 4,223 on March 13. This was a sudden step change, not a gradual ramp.

## What Changed

The specific changes haven't been identified from our benchmarking data alone. The jump coincides with a mori-sglang update that improved FP4 disaggregated prefill scheduling on AMD MI355X hardware.

## Competitive Context

Before this update, MI355X's best DeepSeek-R1-0528 result was 1,893 tok/s/GPU, putting it at 14% of the NVIDIA B200's 13,430 tok/s/GPU. After the update, MI355X reaches 4,223 tok/s/GPU, now at 31% of the B200. Still a large gap, but this is the fastest improvement rate we've seen on AMD hardware for this model. At $0.10/Mtok vs B200's $0.04/Mtok, MI355X needs to close the throughput gap further to compete on cost.
