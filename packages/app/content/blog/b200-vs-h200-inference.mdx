NVIDIA B200 vs NVIDIA H200 across 7 shared models on [InferenceX](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com). B200 wins on throughput in all 7. It also wins on cost per Mtok in all 7. This is a generational gap, not a close race.

## Key Numbers

- B200 leads on gpt-oss-120b: **61,612.7 tok/s/GPU** vs H200's 15,202.1, a 4.1x difference.
- On DeepSeek-R1-0528, the gap is 5.5x: 13,429.7 vs 2,453.9 tok/s/GPU.
- Closest model is MiniMax-M2.5: B200 at 9,652.0 vs H200 at 3,370.8 tok/s/GPU, still 2.9x.
- B200 is cheaper per Mtok on every shared model. On DeepSeek-R1-0528, it's $0.04 vs $0.16.

## B200 vs H200 on DeepSeek-R1-0528

B200 gets 13,429.7 tok/s/GPU, 5.5x H200's 2,453.9. This is the widest gap in the dataset. B200 runs this 671B MoE model in FP4 on TensorRT-LLM with disaggregated prefill. H200's best result is FP8 on SGLang. The combination of FP4 quantization, a newer framework, and Blackwell's memory bandwidth creates a large compounding advantage.

## How wide is the gap across models?

It varies. gpt-oss-120b: 4.1x. Llama-3.3-70B: 2.5x. MiniMax-M2.5: 2.9x. Qwen-3.5-397B-A17B: 3.2x. The gap is smallest on dense 70B-class models and widest on large MoE architectures. If you're running any model at scale, B200 is the clear upgrade from H200.
