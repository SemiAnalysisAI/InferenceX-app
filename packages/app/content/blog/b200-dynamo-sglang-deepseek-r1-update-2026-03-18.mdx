NVIDIA B200 on dynamo-sglang FP8 with disaggregated prefill went from 2,237 to 4,242 tok/s/GPU on DeepSeek-R1-0528, a 1.9x gain in five weeks. TTFT dropped from 58,236ms to 8,254ms at the same time.

## Before/After

|                        |        Before |         After | Change |
| ---------------------- | ------------: | ------------: | -----: |
| Throughput/GPU (tok/s) |         2,237 |         4,242 |   +90% |
| Median TTFT (ms)       |        58,236 |         8,254 |   -86% |
| Median TPOT (ms)       |          19.9 |          52.4 |      - |
| Framework              | dynamo-sglang | dynamo-sglang |      - |
| Concurrency            |           512 |         1,024 |      - |
| Date                   |    2026-02-13 |    2026-03-18 |      - |

TPOT rose from 19.9ms to 52.4ms because the after config runs at 2x the concurrency. The TTFT improvement is the standout here: 86% lower, even at higher batch size.

The dynamo-sglang FP8 disagg line shows steady growth from 2,037 in early February to 4,242 on March 18. Other B200 configs also improved over this period: trt FP4 jumped from ~8,400 to 10,505 tok/s/GPU, and sglang FP8 climbed from ~1,480 to 2,265. The dynamo-trt FP4 disagg config at 13,430 tok/s/GPU still leads on this GPU.

## What Changed

[PR #907](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/907) replaced the old per-file recipes for B200 FP8 DSR1 8k1k dynamo-sglang with resolved variants from a consolidated config. The new config has 14 variants covering STP/MTP at both low-latency and max-throughput, with updated concurrency points and scale settings.

## Competitive Context

This moves dynamo-sglang FP8 disagg from 7th to 6th among B200 configs on DeepSeek-R1. The B200's best config is still dynamo-trt FP4 disagg at 13,430 tok/s/GPU, so FP8 on dynamo-sglang is at 32% of peak B200 throughput. Against other GPUs at FP8, the B200 now beats the H200's best FP8 result (2,454 tok/s/GPU on trt) by 73%. At $0.13/Mtok for dynamo-sglang FP8 disagg vs the H200's $0.16/Mtok, it's cheaper per token too.
