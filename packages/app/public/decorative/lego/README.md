# LEGO theme assets

Decorative files for the optional LEGO theme (`components/lego/`). Nothing in this folder is
requested unless a visitor picks the LEGO theme; audio is fetched on first use.

Notes on the sources:

- The spoken lines and the "Everything Is Awesome" excerpt come from The LEGO Movie (Warner Bros. / WaterTower Music).
- The build, break, fall-apart and stud chimes are user uploads on Myinstants that are titled as
  LEGO video-game sounds. They are rips, not official stems. `stud.mp3` carried the metadata
  title "Up Purple Studs (SFX)" from a YouTube rip.
- The rioforce brick recordings are Freesound preview MP3s from [pack 14369](https://freesound.org/people/rioforce/packs/14369/). The pack is listed as CC0.
- Minifigure and part photographs are BrickLink catalogue images, the spaceship, helicopter and police station photographs are Brickset set images, the Dexter's Diner picture is a game screenshot hosted on the LEGO Games Wiki (Fandom), and the two character renders are hosted on Brickipedia (Fandom).
- The spoken clips were identified by speech-to-text and by the source page titles, not by ear.
- The background music is a cut of the instrumental of "Everything Is Awesome" taken from an Internet Archive upload of the film's soundtrack.
- The LEGO City advert narration, the LEGO Star Wars Yoda and minikit sounds and the LEGO Island
  line are Myinstants user uploads.
- The helicopter photograph shows set 7903. Brickipedia says the advert behind the "A man has
  fallen into the river in LEGO City" meme was for this set; Know Your Meme dates the advert to
  2009 and names no set number. Which set the advert showed was not checked against the video.

No file here is AI generated or redrawn. Studs, bricks, plates and the baseplate that the theme
draws in CSS, SVG and canvas are plain geometric shapes made in code and are not listed.

The background-removal step is a mask only: pixels of the figure are never repainted.

The theme's background music is `audio/everything-is-awesome.mp3`, played as a loop with WebAudio. It is fetched only while the LEGO theme is active, the music switch is on and the visitor has clicked, tapped or pressed a key. The source file is named `28 Everything Is Awesome!!! (Instrum.mp3`; the cut was chosen from the waveform and tempo and has not been listened to.

`audio/destroy-you.mp3` (a threatening movie line used on game over) was removed in this round;
Brick Stacker now plays `audio/yoda-death.mp3` there.

The stud rows, the stepped brick corners on cards, the corner towers and the toppled tower in
empty states are abstract SVG shapes (rectangles, ellipses, gradients) written by a small
script and embedded in `lego-theme.css` as data URIs. They are not traced from, or cut out of,
any LEGO image, and they are not files in this folder.
