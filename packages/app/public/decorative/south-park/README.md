# South Park theme assets

The character masters and locations are the images identified in South Park Studios' [character catalog](https://southpark.cc.com/w/index.php/List_of_Characters), [Stan](https://southpark.cc.com/w/index.php/Stan), [Kyle](https://southpark.cc.com/w/index.php/Kyle_Broflovski), [Cartman](https://southpark.cc.com/w/index.php/Eric_Cartman), [Kenny](https://southpark.cc.com/w/index.php/Kenny_McCormick) and [location catalog](https://southpark.cc.com/w/index.php/List_of_Locations). The original wiki's `southparkstudios.mtvnimages.com` host is retired; the links below are the corresponding Paramount image masters. No characters were generated, traced or redrawn.

## Theme-local runtime artwork and sound

2026-11-26: The dispatch arcade reuses the documented studio WebPs without new character artwork or transformations. `route-world.ts`, `defense-engine.ts`, `route-atlas.tsx`, `charts.ts` and the two theme stylesheets implement original geometric scenery, buildings, vehicles, paper chart marks, the route map and UI decoration directly in Canvas/SVG/CSS. `paper-synth.ts` generates original plucked tones and paper percussion at runtime; it does not sample a recording. The menu, route rules and bilingual copy live in `bus-stop-arcade.tsx`, `arcade-copy.ts`, `defense-routes.ts`, `case-drawer.tsx` and `preferences.ts`. No new image, font or audio file was imported.

The town reel has five saved settings: Snow Day, Night Watch, Elementary, Stark’s Pond and Casa Dusk. Scene, copy, paper colors and procedural town arrangements follow the selected chip. The radio’s speaker mutes both voice and music; the music button retains the separate soundtrack preference. The town loop resumes its beat on in-app navigation.

The coroner drawer counts distinct incidents, not repeated clicks. Eight of twelve open the costume trunk; equipping it changes the four boys on the site. In the arcade, the dispatch bell calls the next scheduled group only when the previous group has left. A stop-one transfer funds the workshop; two cache ranks add an optional once-per-stop reserve vent. The Lab exports the stop, seed and four lane stations as a transfer code; it does not import campaign scores or permits.

Round 3 adds the studio’s three costumed boys for the eight-case night patrol. They are trimmed source cutouts, not drawings. The Chinese display face is used for selected headings and scene controls; ordinary prose retains the system CJK fallback.
