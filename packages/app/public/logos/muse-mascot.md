# Muse mascot asset

`muse-mascot.webp` is a 128 × 128 downsample of the mascot portrait served by
[Muse](https://muse.ai) at
https://muse.ai/images/landing/brand/hatch.jpg, retrieved October 15, 2026.
It preserves the source colors and uses rounded transparent corners. The artwork
belongs to its respective owner and identifies Muse Spark Open; it is not a
replacement for Meta's corporate mark on other models or partner surfaces.
